# Spin Glasses: A Statistical Exploration

An interactive exploration of spin glasses from the perspective of mathematical statistics, probability theory, and statistical modelling.

The project focuses on the statistical structure of disordered systems, using interactive visualizations and computational experiments to explore:

### Areas of Exploration
- Random variables and probability distributions
- Random interactions and dependence structures
- Statistical mechanics and Gibbs distributions
- Correlation functions and order parameters
- Monte Carlo methods and stochastic simulation
- Energy landscapes and non-convex optimization
- High-dimensional statistical systems

### Objective
To explore how randomness, interactions, and statistical dependence give rise to complex collective behaviour, and to investigate the connections between spin glass theory and modern statistical methods.

**Built with:** React, JavaScript, and interactive data visualization libraries.
